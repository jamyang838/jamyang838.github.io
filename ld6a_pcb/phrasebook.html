整趟最重要的一句話

“Neither terminal of the diode is at ground. If the cathode touches ground, the diode is destroyed.”

LD 的兩端都不是地。陰極碰到地，LD 就會燒掉。這句話在見面、接線、上電前各講一次。

3Explaining the board介紹這塊板子

3.1 The 30-second introduction ★30 秒介紹（整段背起來）

This is the LD-PULSE-6A, revision B0. It's a constant-current pulse driver for a single 793-nanometre F-mount pump diode. It delivers up to six amps in flat-top pulses — five hundred microseconds at up to eight hundred hertz — with rise and fall times of about thirty microseconds and no overshoot on the leading edge. It runs from twelve volts, draws less than one amp on average, and you control it over a USB serial console. For safety, it has a three-path hardware interlock, and it can only be armed with the button on the board.

這是 LD-PULSE-6A rev B0，給單顆 793 nm F-mount 泵浦二極體用的恆流脈衝驅動板。最大 6 A 平頂脈衝，500 µs、最高 800 Hz，上升下降約 30 µs、前緣無過衝。12 V 供電、平均不到 1 A，用 USB 序列埠主控台控制。安全上有三路硬體互鎖，而且只能用板上按鍵解鎖。

3.2 The two-minute walkthrough 兩分鐘講解電路（邊指板子邊講）

Power input
The twelve-volt input goes through a resettable fuse, a TVS diode and a P-channel MOSFET for reverse-polarity protection. A buck converter then steps it down to a rail we call V-power, between two point four and four volts. That's the anode voltage.
12 V 經自復保險絲、TVS、P-MOS 反接保護，再由 buck 降到 VPWR 2.4–4.0 V，也就是 LD 陽極電壓。
Current loop
The diode sits between V-power and the drain of Q1, the pass transistor. An op-amp drives Q1's gate so that the voltage across a twenty-milliohm Kelvin sense resistor equals the setpoint. So six amps corresponds to a hundred and twenty millivolts.
LD 接在 VPWR 與 Q1 汲極之間。運放驅動 Q1 閘極，讓 20 mΩ 凱文取樣電阻上的電壓等於設定值：6 A = 120 mV。
Setpoint
The setpoint is a ten-bit PWM from the microcontroller. It goes through a gate powered from a precision two-point-five-volt reference, then an RC filter and a divider — so it's basically a PWM DAC with an accurate amplitude.
設定值來自 MCU 的 10-bit PWM，經 2.5 V 基準供電的閘、RC 濾波與分壓，等於一個振幅精準的 PWM DAC。
Vds servo
If we dropped twelve volts linearly, Q1 would burn about sixty watts. Instead, the firmware measures Q1's drain-source voltage on every pulse and trims the buck output so that V-D-S stays around six hundred millivolts. At full load, Q1 dissipates only about one and a half watts on average.
12 V 直接線性降壓 Q1 要燒 60 W。所以韌體每發量 Q1 的 Vds，調整 buck 輸出讓 Vds 維持約 600 mV；滿載時 Q1 平均只有約 1.5 W。
Bias
Between pulses we keep a small sub-threshold bias — fifty milliamps by default. That's far below the threshold of about point seven amps, so there's no emission, but the loop stays closed. That's why the rise time is about thirty microseconds and doesn't drift with temperature.
脈衝間維持 50 mA 次閾值偏置（門檻約 0.7 A，不出光），迴路一直閉合，所以上升約 30 µs 而且不隨溫度漂移。
Gating & interlock
There are two gating paths. Each pulse is gated by shunting the setpoint. Arming and the interlock clamp Q1's gate and reset the integrator. If the interlock opens, three independent hardware paths shut the output down — the firmware isn't involved.
兩路閘控：每一發由分流設定值控制；解鎖與互鎖則鉗住 Q1 閘極並復位積分器。互鎖一開，三路硬體同時切斷，不經韌體。
Hardware limit & faults
There's also a hardware pulse-width limit of about one point two milliseconds, so even if the firmware hangs, a single pulse can't last longer than that. On top of that, the firmware checks for three faults — no current, overheating, and interlock — each with its own blink code.
另有約 1.2 ms 的硬體脈寬上限，韌體當機也不會更長。韌體另外守三種故障：無電流、過熱、互鎖，各有閃碼。

3.3 Key points in one sentence 重點一句話

★This is a low-side regulator, so the cathode is not at ground — it sits on the drain of Q1.這是低側恆流，陰極不是地，而是在 Q1 汲極。
★Please don't connect either diode terminal to ground or to the heat sink.請不要把 LD 任一端接到地或散熱座。
The edges get slower at lower current, because the loop gain goes with the square root of the current.電流越低邊沿越慢，因為迴路增益隨電流平方根變化。
Below about three amps, the edges are slower than fifty microseconds.低於約 3 A 時，邊沿會慢於 50 µs。
The maximum load voltage is about three volts at six amps — enough for one diode, not two in series.6 A 時負載電壓最高約 3 V，夠一顆 LD，不能兩顆串聯。
The pulse width is defined as the FWHM above the bias pedestal.脈寬定義為偏置基座之上的半高寬。

5Questions they will ask對方可能會問的問題

回答原則：先給結論（一句話），再給理由，最後給數字。不知道就說不知道，回去查了再回覆。

Why do you need a bias current between pulses?
It keeps the current loop closed between pulses. Without it, every pulse has to start from zero and the op-amp first has to charge Q1's gate up to threshold. With fifty milliamps of bias, the rise time at six amps is about thirty microseconds, and it doesn't drift with temperature or with the MOSFET.
讓迴路在脈衝間保持閉合；否則每發都要先把 Q1 閘極充到門檻。50 mA 偏置下 6 A 上升約 30 µs，不隨溫度與元件漂移。
Can you switch the bias off?
Yes, with B 0. Then the firmware compensates for the dead zone, but the start-up delay goes up to about fifty-six microseconds, it varies with temperature, and at five hundred microseconds the minimum current is three point four amps.
可以，B 0。韌體改用死區補償，但起步延遲約 56 µs 且隨溫度變；W 500 時最小電流 3.4 A。
Is fifty milliamps really safe for the diode?
It's far below the threshold of about point seven amps, so there's no lasing, and it adds less than a tenth of a watt. It's adjustable from zero to two hundred milliamps if you want it lower.
遠低於約 0.7 A 門檻，不出光，多耗不到 0.1 W；可在 0–200 mA 調整。
Why isn't the cathode grounded?
Because the regulator is on the low side: the MOSFET and the sense resistor sit between the cathode and ground. That gives us a ground-referenced sense resistor, which keeps the current loop simple, fast and accurate. The trade-off is that neither diode terminal can touch ground.
因為是低側恆流：MOSFET 和取樣電阻在陰極與地之間。取樣電阻以地為參考，迴路簡單、快、準；代價是 LD 兩端都不能碰地。
How accurate is the current?
After calibration the flat-top is within plus or minus one percent, and pulse-to-pulse it's better than a tenth of a percent. Each board was calibrated individually. Today's comparison with the Picolas will be the independent check.
校準後平頂 ±1 %，逐發 < 0.1 %。每片個別校準；今天和 Picolas 對照就是獨立驗證。
What are the rise times at lower current?
About thirty and thirty-three microseconds at six amps, forty and forty-five at three amps, and sixty-two and seventy-two at one amp. The loop gain scales with the square root of the current, so lower currents are slower.
6 A 約 30/33 µs、3 A 約 39/45、1 A 約 62/72 µs。迴路增益隨電流平方根，所以低電流較慢。
Can it run CW?
No. The duty cycle is limited to forty-two percent. At five hundred microseconds that means up to about eight hundred and twenty-five hertz.
不行，工作週期上限 42 %；W 500 µs 時最高約 825 Hz。
Can we run it from our 24-volt supply?
No, please don't. It's twelve volts plus or minus ten percent only. The buck converter's absolute maximum is seventeen volts.
不行。只能 12 V ±10 %，buck 絕對上限 17 V。
Can we arm it from our control software?
Not in this revision. That was a deliberate safety decision: you can change all settings over USB, but arming needs a person to press the button. If you need remote arming, we can discuss it for the next revision.
這版不行，是刻意的安全設計：設定都能用 USB 改，但解鎖要有人按鍵。需要遠端解鎖的話，下一版可以討論。
What happens if the interlock opens in the middle of a pulse?
The current ramps down through the setpoint path at the loop's speed and falls below ten percent in about thirty-five microseconds. The hard clamps are delayed by about a tenth of a millisecond on purpose; otherwise the cable inductance would put a reverse voltage on the diode.
電流先經設定值路徑以迴路速度降下，約 35 µs 內到 10 % 以下。硬鉗位刻意延後約 0.1 ms，否則線纜電感會讓 LD 承受反壓。
Can it drive two diodes in series?
No. The compliance is about three volts at six amps — enough for one diode, but two in series would need more than three point three volts.
不行。6 A 時順從電壓約 3 V，一顆夠，兩顆串聯要超過 3.3 V。
How long can the cable be? Can we add a capacitor across the diode?
Up to one metre of twisted pair — about one microhenry. Please don't put more than one nanofarad across the diode, and no series components; the loop was designed for that.
雙絞線最長 1 m（約 1 µH）。LD 兩端不要並 > 1 nF，也不要串元件；迴路是照這個條件設計的。
How do you define the pulse width? And what's the jitter?
Pulse width is the full width at half maximum above the bias pedestal; after calibration it equals the W setting. Internal triggering has less than one microsecond of jitter. External triggering has a delay of about six microseconds and up to about six microseconds of jitter.
脈寬 = 偏置基座之上的半高寬，校準後等於 W。內部觸發抖動 < 1 µs；外部觸發延遲約 6 µs，抖動最多約 6 µs。
How much power does it draw?
About point four amps from twelve volts at four hundred hertz, and point seven to point eight amps at eight hundred hertz, at six amps and five hundred microseconds. During the pulse itself it can draw up to about two amps, so the adapter should be rated for at least two amps.
6 A、500 µs 時：400 Hz 約 0.4 A、800 Hz 約 0.7–0.8 A。脈衝中瞬間可到約 2 A，變壓器要 ≥ 2 A。
Has this been tested with a real diode?
Not yet — today is the first time. All boards were tested and calibrated on a dummy load in Taiwan, including the interlock test with a one-microhenry cable. That's why we start at one amp.
還沒，今天是第一次。所有板子都在台灣用假負載測試校準過，包括 1 µH 線纜下的互鎖測試，所以我們從 1 A 開始。
Why not just use a linear regulator from 12 V?
Q1 would dissipate around sixty watts at six amps. With the buck and the Vds servo, it's about one and a half watts average at eight hundred hertz.
6 A 時 Q1 要燒約 60 W；用 buck 加 Vds 伺服，800 Hz 時平均約 1.5 W。
What if it overheats?
The firmware shuts down if the estimated Q1 power stays above three watts for five seconds, or if the board reaches eighty-five degrees. Above thirty degrees ambient at eight hundred hertz, we recommend a small fan.
Q1 估算功耗 > 3 W 持續 5 秒或板溫 85 °C 時韌體停機。環境 > 30 °C 且 800 Hz 時建議加小風扇。

6Survival phrases溝通救命句

技術內容你都懂，卡住通常是因為沒聽清楚或一時想不到字。這些句子讓你爭取時間、確認理解，而且聽起來很專業。

6.1 When you didn't catch it 沒聽清楚時

★Sorry, could you say that again a bit more slowly?不好意思，可以說慢一點再講一次嗎？
★Just to make sure I understand: you'd like six amps at four hundred hertz, right?確認一下我的理解：你要 6 A、400 Hz，對嗎？
Do you mean the anode or the cathode?你是指陽極還是陰極？
Was that fifteen — one-five — or fifty — five-zero?是 15 還是 50？
Could you write that number down for me?可以把那個數字寫給我嗎？
Sorry, I'm not familiar with that term. What does it mean?抱歉，我不熟那個詞，是什麼意思？
Could you show me on the schematic?可以在原理圖上指給我看嗎？

6.2 Buying time & being honest 爭取時間、誠實回答

Good question. Let me think about that for a second.好問題，讓我想一下。
Let me check my notes. / Give me a minute to look at the schematic.我查一下筆記 / 給我一分鐘看原理圖。
★I'm not sure off the top of my head. I'll check and get back to you by email.我一時不確定，查了再用 email 回覆你。
That number is from simulation; we haven't measured it with a real diode yet.那是模擬值，還沒用真的 LD 量過。
I'm not 100 % sure, but I think it's around thirty microseconds.我不是百分之百確定，但應該約 30 µs。
Let me draw a quick sketch — it's easier to explain that way.我畫個簡圖，這樣比較好解釋。
How do you say … in English? The thing that … ……英文怎麼說？就是那個用來……的東西。

6.3 Suggesting, agreeing, disagreeing 提議、同意、委婉不同意

How about we try it at one amp first?我們先用 1 A 試試看如何？
Shall we take a short break and look at the data?要不要休息一下看看數據？
I'd suggest we check the polarity once more before arming.我建議解鎖前再確認一次極性。
That makes sense. / Good point. / I agree.有道理 / 好觀點 / 我同意。
I see your point, but I'd rather not skip the dummy-load check, because …我懂你的意思，但我不想跳過假負載確認，因為……
★I'd prefer not to go above six amps — the board isn't specified for that.我不想超過 6 A，板子不是為那個設計的。
I'm afraid that's not possible with this revision.恐怕這一版做不到。
Let's note that down as an open point.把這個記成待辦事項。

6.4 Asking for help & checking before acting 請求協助、動手前確認

Could we borrow a current-limited bench supply?可以借一台可限流的實驗室電源嗎？
Do you have a one-to-one probe and a ground spring?有 1:1 探棒和彈簧地嗎？
Where can I plug in the adapter? / Is this socket 230 volts?變壓器插哪裡？這個插座是 230 V 嗎？
★Is it OK if I power it up now?我現在可以上電嗎？
Can I touch this, or is it live?這可以碰嗎，還是有電？
Could you hold this probe for a moment?可以幫我拿一下探棒嗎？
Is it OK to take photos of the setup?可以拍設備照片嗎？

跟德國工程師溝通的小提醒
  • 德國同事講英文通常很直接，「No, that won't work.」不是不禮貌，照技術內容回應即可。
