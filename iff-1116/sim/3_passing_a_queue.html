Passing a queue at a counter

Scenarios

The robot makes a round trip to a pick-up point. A queue waiting at a counter stands across its path; people are served and leave, and new people join the end.

What to watch APlans with the people it senses within 7 m and treats the gaps between them as free space, so on the way out it passes through the queue. BReceives every person’s position and which people form the queue, and goes around the end of the queue.
